Project Core Idea:
The core idea of this project is combining glitch, memes and horror together.
In my index.html page, I decide to make it similar to a warning sign from a military base or a restricted area, warning people who open this web-art work maybe dangerous and they might see some horror videos and some viral Italian brainrot characters. To make it more similar to the vibe of a military base or a restricted area, I added some very noisy alarm sound to make it more realistic. Also the structure of my index.html page is based to the concept of what I learnt in Week 7 workshop.
In my page-1.html, I use the idea of JODI's website. In this page I want to put a lot of scary video's or photos inside the Ascii, so when a user is exploring my web-art work, they click inside and random videos or photos pop up and jummp scare the users. And also for the title I make it as "I've already told you not to click me" just to make it feel like there is an eye watching you the whole time in the dark, creating a spooky vibe. Also, in this page I want to prove that Jodi is right about glitch is not a failure but as a strategy for critical reflection.
In my page-2.html, the core idea is just trolling. Cause after exploring my page-1.html, user may already be scared to click on another page, they will be cautious the whole time. So I was thinking why not just leave this page to look empty and hide some hints to let them explore by themselves. After users find my hints they will be thinking is there any scary videos or photos poping up. However, the reality is there's nothing in this page, they are getting trolled. In order to make it more trolling, I added classic elevator waiting music in the background. Also, same as page-1.html I make this page title as "Did you really click on me again?", just to continue the spooky feeling of someone watching you in the dark.
In my page-3.html, the idea was actually putting some viral meme characters in 2025 to let user to know more about them. I think memes are funny, you always see them on Instagram reels or TikTok. This year, brainrot memes become so viral especially the Italian Brainrot meme, so I want people to know more about these meme chracters and their origins. I also add a song from these memes to make it more funny.
